Why no to Graph?
================
Show a sample $graphLookup pipeline and explain exactly where it blows up

Blow-up #1: $graphLookup fan-out (User → Groups)
    Typical enterprise user:
        10–50 groups (often more)
        No hard upper bound
MongoDB behavior:
    $graphLookup materializes the entire traversal in memory
    Every group membership becomes a document in groupMemberships

At scale:
500K users × 20 groups = 10M edges
Even for one user, this step can already return dozens of documents.
